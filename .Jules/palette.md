## 2025-06-10 - [Accessibility and Feedback in Vision Mode]
**Learning:** Icon-only buttons and state-switching controls must include `aria-label`, `aria-pressed` (if applicable), and `focus-visible` ring styles to maintain high standards of keyboard accessibility and provide clear feedback for screen readers.
**Action:** Always pair `aria-label` with `focus-visible` ring styles on icon-only buttons, and use `aria-pressed` for toggleable tabs or modes.

## 2025-05-14 - [AI Vision Accessibility & Utility]
**Learning:** Adding a "Copy to Clipboard" feature to AI analysis results significantly improves the utility of the Vision mode, while implementing a full suite of ARIA attributes (role="group", aria-label, aria-pressed) and focus-visible indicators ensures the feature is accessible to all users.
**Action:** Always pair new interaction features with appropriate ARIA state management and keyboard focus indicators to maintain the project's accessibility baseline.

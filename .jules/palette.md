## 2025-05-22 - Standardizing Modal Accessibility
**Learning:** Modals without proper ARIA roles and labels are invisible to screen readers. Using 'role="dialog"', 'aria-modal="true"', and 'aria-labelledby' with unique IDs from 'useId' ensures the modal's purpose is correctly announced.
**Action:** Implement standardized ARIA attributes and focus-visible rings in the Modal component.

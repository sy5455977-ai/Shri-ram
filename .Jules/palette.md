## 2025-06-06 - Accessibility and Functional Delight in AI Vision
**Learning:** For technical AI features like OCR and Math analysis, provide a one-click 'Copy' action with immediate visual feedback. Always pair icon-only buttons with `aria-label` and `focus-visible` rings to ensure the high-tech UI remains accessible to all users.
**Action:** Implement 'Copy' buttons with `Check` icon feedback and standardized ARIA labels for all analysis/output components.

## 2025-05-14 - Enhancing Vision Mode Accessibility and Utility
**Learning:** Users analyzing images often need to export or use the results elsewhere. Providing a quick "Copy" button directly in the result header significantly reduces friction. Pairing this with `aria-label` and `focus-visible` ensures the new feature is accessible to all users.
**Action:** Always include copy-to-clipboard functionality for AI-generated text results and ensure icon-only buttons have descriptive ARIA labels.

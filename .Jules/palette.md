## 2026-06-13 - [VisionMode Accessibility & Utility]
**Learning:** Adding ARIA attributes (label, pressed) and focus-visible states to icon-only buttons significantly improves keyboard and screen-reader accessibility in vision-based AI components. Providing immediate visual feedback (icon toggle) for clipboard actions enhances the 'delight' factor of micro-interactions.
**Action:** Always include ARIA labels for capture/reset buttons and utilize aria-pressed for mode switchers to communicate state programmatically.

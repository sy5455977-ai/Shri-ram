## 2025-05-14 - Vision Mode Accessibility & Copy Pattern
**Learning:** Adding semantic grouping (role="group") and toggle states (aria-pressed) to task switchers significantly improves screen reader navigation. A "Copy to Clipboard" button in the analysis results is a high-value UX win for productivity tools.
**Action:** Always include aria-pressed for mode switchers and prioritize descriptive aria-labels for icon-only buttons like camera controls.
